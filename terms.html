01Using ScamSense

ScamSense is a scam-protection app built by Karim Haidari. By using it, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

ScamSense is a prototype. Features may change, pause, or stop without notice.

02Warnings, not guarantees

ScamSense uses built-in rules and AI to judge whether something looks like a scam. Scammers change their tricks all the time, and AI can make mistakes. So:

  • A "Safe" result does not promise that something is safe.
  • A "Scam" result can sometimes be wrong about a real message.
  • Always use your own judgment, and check with your bank, the company, or someone you trust before sending money or personal information.

03Not professional advice

ScamSense and its helper Mahi give general safety information. They are not legal, financial, medical, or law-enforcement advice. The report documents ScamSense helps you create are drafts. Read them before you send them. For legal or financial decisions, talk to a qualified professional.

04Your email accounts

If you connect Gmail or Outlook, you allow ScamSense to read your email to look for scams. If you tap Move to Spam or Block sender, you allow it to move that email and to create a filter or rule for that sender. You can undo a block in Settings, and you can remove ScamSense's access at any time from your Google or Microsoft account.

Only connect email accounts that belong to you, or that you have permission to manage.

  • Use it to access someone else's accounts or information without permission.

The shared AI helper has a daily limit for everyone. We may limit or block use that harms the service or other people.
